Retention Trackers & Sending Rules

Wednesday · Orange Flag

Zero sessions so far this week

  • Head Coach reviews 10:15–10:45am. Send only after sign-off and REFRESH AFTER REVIEW.
  • If no Head Coach response by noon, hold the SMS and contact the Operations Manager via Slack for approval.
  • Kickstarter members: Head Coach calls only, never SMS. Sponsored members: no SMS or call. Respect all Head Coach removals.
  • Repeat Orange Flag members (column P): ask the Head Coach whether a personal call is needed.
  • Choose one of the five approved templates. Replace [Name] where present. Send by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Log Reached Out By, Type, Replied? and Coming Back? in the weekly tracker.

Monday · Red Flag

Zero attendance in the last 7 days

  • Tracker ready before noon. Head Coach reviews removals; click REFRESH AFTER REVIEW.
  • Send only after the Head Coach confirms the Messaging Owner (column L) and message approach.
  • Repeat Red Flag members (column V): Head Coach confirms the approach before any standard template is used.
  • Exclude all Kickstarter and Sponsored memberships. Exclude Thailand Tour only when it is the member’s sole membership.
  • Choose one adult template per member, or one parent template per family. Replace all name placeholders and send via Clubworx SMS.
  • Log Reached Out By, Type, Replied? and Coming Back? in the tracker.
